# S1-LXF-BAR v1.1 results: level × bar-flow Bayes on public BTCUSDT klines

**PAPER ONLY.** No orders, no sizing, no rates beyond the spec's fee placeholders. Every number below was computed by `code/s1/s1.py` from checksum-verified `data.binance.vision` files, and is traceable to a CSV in this folder. Spec: [`20261003-s1-level-x-bar-flow-spec.md`](../../20261003-s1-level-x-bar-flow-spec.md) v1.1. Run on 2026-10-03; the test split was computed once, at 12:35 UTC.

## Decision-table row reached: H1 Pass, H2 Fail, G Fail

> **"The level leans but bar flow adds nothing at 1 m. S2 must show the interaction at 1 s before any DOM work is justified."**

| Test | Result | What decided it | Sample |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1-H1** level lean | **PASS** | 4 of 6 develop lean cells hold on validate (one-sided p < 0.05) and on test (same side, n ≥ 15) | Real `CLOCK_OK` events with a RESPECT/BREAK label: 2,352 develop, 655 validate, 416 test |
| **S1-H2** flow confirms more at real than placebo levels | **FAIL** | The lower 97.5% bound of I is below 0 for both RESPECT and BREAK on validate and on test | 767 validate and 472 test real `CLOCK_OK` events; 1,358 and 618 primary placebo events |
| **S1-G** AGREE clears fees at 3 m | **FAIL** | n_AGREE is 16 on validate (needs ≥ 30) and 5 on test (needs ≥ 15); the validate 95% lower bound is −2.85 bp; July supplies 90% of pooled ΣY_net | 36 / 16 / 5 AGREE events |

**Read the H1 pass with a large caveat.** Placebo levels 25 and 50 bp away respect at the same rate as the real VWAP and IB levels, or higher: 0.705 against 0.699 on develop, 0.730 against 0.711 on validate, and 0.730 against 0.647 on test. The roughly 70% RESPECT rate is the geometry of the label, not a property of the levels. A touch that stops up to 5 bp short of the level is already closer to the +10 bp RESPECT barrier than to the −10 bp BREAK barrier. H1 tests against a coin flip, so it passes on that geometry. The level model also does not beat a constant: validate log loss 0.612 for the beta-binomial cells and 0.602 for the logistic cross-check (`summary.json`), against 0.601 for the develop base rate (the 2,352 develop events give a RESPECT share of 0.6986; scoring the 655 validate events with that constant, from `events.parquet`). **This run found no level-specific lean and no level-specific flow confirmation.** The section on what this changes below proposes how S1 v1.2 should pose H1.

## v1.1 robustness rows from iteration 01

**R-OI (proposal C2, "loaded" absorption).** The data point the opposite way to C2. RESPECT confirmations with OI rising do better than the non-confirmed baseline (Δ_real +0.87, +1.39 and +0.44 bp on develop, validate and test), while those with OI flat or falling do worse (−0.65, −1.12, −1.11). ## Implementation choices where the spec is silent

These were fixed in code before any result was read. None changes a spec value.

| Topic | Choice |
|---|---|
| Beta-binomial prior | Method of moments on develop cell shares within level type. The intra-class correlation is ρ = (s²/(μ(1−μ)) − mean(1/n)) / (1 − mean(1/n)), clipped to [10⁻⁶, 1 − 10⁻⁶], with α + β = 1/ρ − 1 and μ the pooled share |
| `EDGE_OK` candidates | Develop gate events in the lean cell that pass `CLOCK_OK`, `INDEPENDENT_AGREE` (with `EPISODE_ONCE` applied to pre-`EDGE_OK` passes) and `PRINT_CONFIRM`. This follows the program's "held-out lower bound of the markout for this gate cell". A cell with no candidates fails. The all-events reading is reported as a sensitivity |
| Interval bounds | "95% lower bound" is the 2.5th percentile of the day-block bootstrap; the 97.5% interval runs from the 1.25th to the 98.75th percentile. B = 2,000, seed 20261003, one weight matrix per split shared by real and placebo statistics |
| Clusters | Real events at the same bar whose levels are within 5 bp pairwise, joined into connected components. The gate representative is chosen by `CLOCK_OK`, then a non-NONE hypothesis, then VWAP > IBH > IBL > IBM, then UTC. Conflict means more than one distinct (H_L, s) among `CLOCK_OK` members. An AGREE spends every member's instance |
| `EPISODE_ONCE` | An instance is spent by its first AGREE |
| G concentration | Checked on validate and test AGREE events pooled. The test split alone is September plus 1 October, so a per-split month rule could never pass |
| `oi_d5c` | Uses the 5-minute grid stamp at or before the decision time; a stamp exactly at the decision time counts. Stamps are complete, so `oi_na` is never set |
| `atsz_t` | The robust z-score of ln(v/n) |
| Placebo hygiene | "Real level" means any real VWAP or IB value of any box that spans bar t |
| 5 m variant | I3 uses 5 m returns. Spot TI is summed from 1 m. The forward horizon is 1 bar (5 minutes), the nearest available to 180 s |

## What this changes

1. **H1 as posed cannot tell a level from the label's geometry.** For S1 v1.2 I propose a placebo-relative H1: real share minus placebo share per cell, with the same day-block bootstrap. Under v1.1 data that difference is about 0 overall and negative on test. This is a proposal for Desk Floor. It is not applied retroactively, and v1.1 stands as reported.
2. **Do not promote any lean cell into a paper gate.** The cells pass a coin-flip test that placebos pass equally.
3. **Bar-resolution flow carries no level-specific information here.** At 1 m, taker imbalance plus close location plus volume z does not confirm better at real levels than at placebo levels, within an MDE of about 2.6–3.8 bp for RESPECT. Per the decision table, **S2 (1 s bars from `trades`, aggTrades for sweep groups only) is the next study, and DOM work stays on hold until S2 reports.**
4. **Iteration 01 proposals.** C2 is withdrawn (it would block every RESPECT AGREE under `LIQ_VETO`). C5 made no difference at 1 m. C7 is untestable at this sample size. C1, C3, C4 and C6 still need S2, S2b and S3 data.
5. **Unchanged:** the locked flag names and order, `PAPER ONLY`, and the BTC time-range job (JOB-20261003-TR-S1), which was not started and still waits on box sign-off.
