## Decision-table row reached: S2-H2 Fail, S2-G Fail, S2-L Fail

> **"The S1 pattern holds at 1 s. Keep the bar model; per the ladder, DOM work must beat S1, not S2. Desk Floor should weigh the program's P1 restatement (a flow model with a session filter) before funding S3. No S2 test split is needed."** (spec §10.2)

**Does moving from 1-minute bars to 1-second bars change the H2 or G failure? No. The same pattern holds.**

- **H2 still fails.** One-second tape confirmations do not predict H-direction 180 s markouts any better at real levels than at placebo levels. On validate the interaction is −0.42 bp for RESPECT and +0.11 bp for BREAK, and both 97.5% intervals contain 0. S1 found +0.14 and −0.65 bp on validate at 1 m.
- **G still fails, for a sharper reason.** The 1 s gate is stricter, so it lets fewer events through. AGREE drops from S1's 16 validate events to **1**, against the n ≥ 30 the rule needs. That one event made +0.02 bp before fees. Loosening the gate does not rescue G. The 8 validate events that pass every flag before `EDGE_OK` average +5.93 bp, but placebo levels under the same rules average +6.05 bp (X3 below), so that gain is not level-specific.
- **Tape adds no lift (S2-L).** Adding the touch-minute tape features to the S1 bar model makes validate log loss slightly worse: ΔLL = −0.0018, 95% interval [−0.0071, 0.0035].

**Why T3b fails, and why it does not change the verdicts.** Spec §4 defines the mid proxy at the end of a second as the average of the latest buyer-initiated and seller-initiated prices if both are at most 10 s old (otherwise the last price, up to 60 s old). Some entry seconds contain prints from only one side. For those, the entry mid reuses an earlier print, which can come from before T_d. That is stale information, not future information, so it is not look-ahead. But it contradicts the T3 wording "perturbing trades before the entry second leaves every label unchanged". T3c confirms that labels never reach back further than the §4 rule allows.

**Sensitivity (not pre-registered, `t3b_sensitivity.csv`).** This check keeps only events whose entry second has at least one buy and one sell print. For those, the entry mid and every later mid use only prints at or after T_d. On validate, 138 of 767 real `CLOCK_OK` events (18.0%) and 204 of 1,358 placebo events (15.0%) have a one-sided entry second on clock B15. With those removed, the verdicts are unchanged:

- S2-H2: RESPECT I = −0.88 bp, 97.5% interval [−3.29, 1.68]; BREAK I = −0.22 [−5.79, 4.70]. Fail.
- S2-G: n_AGREE = 1. Fail.
- S2-L: ΔLL = +0.0008 [−0.0052, 0.0072]. Fail.

A later spec version should either build the entry mid only from prints at or after T_d, or reword T3. Neither change is applied to this run.

**Order of work, for the record.** The first full `s2.py` run printed the three verdicts before `tests_s2.py` existed. After that run the code changed in three ways:

- `episodes.py` gained the T8 check; its outputs are byte-identical.
- `s2.py`'s second-bar builder and label function were refactored so the tests could reuse them.
- `s2.py` gained the T3b sensitivity table.

Every pre-registered output file is byte-identical to that first run's, except `summary.json`, which differs only by the added sensitivity entry.

## S2-H2: does a 1 s confirmation work better at real levels than at placebos?

Clock B15: the decision comes 15 s after the first touching trade. Δ is the mean H-direction `Y1s(180)` of confirmed events minus that of unconfirmed events, for real and for placebo levels. I = Δ_real − Δ_plac, in bp (`h2_interaction.csv`).

| Split | H | Real confirmed / other | Placebo confirmed / other | Δ_real | Δ_plac | I | 97.5% interval | Detectable I (2.8 × SE) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Develop | RESPECT | 1,128 / 1,429 | 1,889 / 2,448 | −0.05 | +0.03 | −0.08 | [−1.56, 1.43] | 1.93 |
| Develop | BREAK | 287 / 2,270 | 550 / 3,787 | +0.16 | +1.27 | −1.11 | [−4.68, 2.19] | 4.21 |
| **Validate** | **RESPECT** | 422 / 345 | 707 / 651 | **−0.42** | +0.00 | **−0.42** | **[−2.53, 1.86]** | 2.72 |
| **Validate** | **BREAK** | 60 / 707 | 133 / 1,225 | **+0.31** | +0.19 | **+0.11** | **[−5.27, 4.87]** | 6.26 |

On validate, a 1 s RESPECT confirmation at a real level is followed by a *worse* RESPECT markout than no confirmation (Δ_real = −0.42 bp). At placebo levels it makes no difference (Δ_plac = +0.00 bp). The data are consistent with no interaction. They put the RESPECT interaction below +1.86 bp at the upper 97.5% bound, but cannot rule out smaller positive values.

How to read it:

- **The label alone (row 1 against row 2)** changes almost nothing. On the minute clock with S1's rule, the tape-built features classify all 20,022 develop and validate events exactly as S1 did (`m60_bar_vs_s1.csv`). On validate that gives 402 / 365 RESPECT and 81 / 686 BREAK confirmations. The same `EDGE_OK` cell and the same AGREE events come out, with identical event ids: 36 develop and 16 validate. Their validate net is +1.32 bp with a lower bound of −2.77, against S1's +1.59 and −2.85. **S1's G failure reproduces under 1 s labels.**

- On the BREAK side, the 1 s rule adds nothing to the bar rule: every bar-rule BREAK confirmation already contained a sweep into the level.

## What this changes

1. **Answer to the S2 question: the S1 pattern holds at 1 s.** Neither H2 nor G changes. H2 stays a null with intervals of similar width. G fails because the finer, stricter gate has almost no events left, and the events a looser gate would add do no better than placebo levels.
2. **Per the decision table:** keep the bar model as the baseline. Any DOM study must beat S1, not S2. Desk Floor should weigh the program's P1 restatement (a flow model with a session filter) before funding S3. **No S2 test split is requested.**
3. **The placebo finding now spans both studies.** S1 found that placebo levels respect as often as real ones. S2 finds that placebo levels under the 1 s rules earn the same markouts as real ones (X3) and that tape lift is no larger at real levels (S2-L). Whatever these rules capture, the S1/S2 level definitions do not localise it.
4. **Spec follow-up (not retroactive):** fix the T3 wording against the §4 mid proxy (above).

## What S2 cannot say

- No quotes and no DOM: quote-walk, spoofing, `DEPTH_AGREE` and true-mid markouts remain untestable. The mid proxy comes from trade prices.
- No fill model: branch B's 0 bp maker fee assumes a fill at the mid proxy.
- `LIQ_VETO` uses 5-minute OI against a 15 s window.
- Episodes are S1's: a touch that S1's 1 m arming rule missed is missed here too.
- Validate stage only. A fail is final under §10.1, so no S2 test split is needed.
